Project Changes
===============

Cursed Bibles
-------------
- Added item models for Cursed Bibles I, II, III, and IV.
- Added matching texture assets for Cursed Bibles I, II, III, and IV.
- Cursed Bibles use the custom CGT TAB.

Sheep Milking
-------------
- Removed Sheep Milk from sheep death drops.
- Adult sheep can be milked by right-clicking with:
  - A glass bottle to receive Sheep Milk.
  - A bowl to receive Sheep Milk Bowl.
  - A bucket to receive Sheep Milk Bucket.
  - A basket to receive Sheep Milk Basket.

Effigy
------
- Added a GUI texture at assets/cgt_mod/textures/gui/effigy.png.
- Updated the Effigy screen to render the texture with the textured GUI shader
  and correct texture dimensions.
- Changed the title and inventory label to Minecraft's conventional GUI text
  color.
- Removed all Effigy versions except the Empty Effigy.

Creative Tab
------------
- Changed the CGT TAB icon to cycle through the Pillager, Player, Skeleton,
  Villager, and Zombie heads on sticks.

- Removed Wedding Ring.
- Removed Diamond Fragment.

Wandering Trader
----------------
- Each wandering trader has a 5% chance to receive an extra trade offer for
  one randomly selected highshroom variant.
- The offer gives one highshroom for a random cost of 1 to 5 emeralds.
- The chance is rolled once per trader and saved to prevent rerolls or
  duplicate offers when the trader is reloaded.
