Project Changes
===============

Cursed Bibles
-------------
- Added item models and matching texture copies for Cursed Bible I,II, III, and IV.

Sheep Milking
-------------
- Removed Sheep Milk from sheep death drops.
- Adult sheep can be milked by right-clicking with:
  - A glass bottle to receive Sheep Milk.
  - A bowl to receive Sheep Milk Bowl.
  - A bucket to receive Sheep Milk Bucket.
  - A basket to receive Sheep Milk Basket.

Effigy Overhaul
----------
- Added a GUI mechanic and texture at assets/cgt_mod/textures/gui/effigy.png.
- Changed the title and inventory text to Minecraft's conventional GUI text color.
- Removed all Effigy version except Empty Effigy.

Item Changes
------------
- Removed Wedding Ring.
- Removed Diamond Fragment.

Wandering Trader
----------------
- Each wandering trader has a 5% chance to receive one additional highshroom
  trade offer. The selected item is randomly chosen from the four highshroom
  variants.
- The offer costs a random 1 to 5 emeralds for one highshroom.
- The roll is stored on the trader to prevent rerolls or duplicate offers
  when the trader is reloaded.
